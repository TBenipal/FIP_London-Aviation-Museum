Welcome To Industry Night 2025

INDUSTRY NIGHT 2025

Join us for an inspiring showcase featuring the creativity and hard work of our IDP3 and UED students, along with Spotlight presentations on the Royal Canadian Air Force Association (Local 427 Wing) project. Students will proudly present their portfolios, highlighting their passion, innovation, and diverse skills across multiple disciplines.

Following the showcase, the celebration continues at the Chef's Table with appetizers, a cash bar, and plenty of opportunities to connect. This is more than just an exhibition it's a chance to network, share feedback, and support the next generation of professionals. Together, let's celebrate talent, creativity, and collaboration while helping these students take the next step in their careers.

INTERACTIVE MEDIA
DESIGN

The Interactive Media Design program at Fanshawe College is a two-year journey into the world of digital creativity, blending design, development, and interactive storytelling. Whether you're passionate about UX/UI, web development, animation, or multimedia production, this program provides a hands-on learning experience that prepares you for a dynamic career in the industry.

Graduates of the Interactive Media Design program are well-equipped for careers in web design, front-end development, digital marketing, graphic design, and beyond. With a strong portfolio of real-world projects, students enter the workforce ready to make an impact!

  • Showcasing the Future of Interactive Media Design

  • Witness the passion and expertise of our students as they present their portfolios, demonstrating their skills across interactive media, UX design, web development, and beyond.

  • Fanshawe Designers Shine in Brothers In Arms Showcase

  • Explore projects from our IDP3 and UED students, featuring Spotlight presentations that highlight the impactful work behind the Brothers In Arms initiative.

  • IMD Students Pay Tribute Through Brothers In Arms

  • Level 4 students collaborated to design and develop a website for the Brothers In Arms Memorial, a tribute by the Srishti Foundation dedicated to honoring Indian and Canadian military veterans in London, ON.
